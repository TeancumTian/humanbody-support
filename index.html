常见问题 FAQ

换了手机或重装后，怎么恢复完整版？

打开首页右上角的齿轮「设置」→「完整版」→「恢复购买」，用当初购买时的 Apple ID 登录 App Store 即可，不会重复扣费。

Open Settings (the gear, top-right of Home) → 完整版 (Full Book) → 恢复购买 (Restore Purchases) while signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with. You won't be charged again.

家人可以一起用吗？

可以，完整版支持「家人共享」。在系统设置里开启家人共享和「购买项目共享」后，家庭成员在自己的设备上就能使用；如果没有马上解锁，点一下「恢复购买」。

Yes — the Full Book supports Family Sharing. With Family Sharing and Purchase Sharing turned on, family members can use it on their own devices; if it doesn't unlock right away, tap Restore Purchases.

阅读进度、书签和笔记能在 iPhone 和 iPad 之间同步吗？

能。设备登录同一个 Apple ID 并打开 iCloud 后，阅读进度、书签、高亮、笔记、自测和记忆卡记录会通过你自己的 iCloud 自动同步。这些数据只在你的设备和你的 iCloud 里，开发者看不到。

Yes. With iCloud on and the same Apple ID, your reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, quiz and flashcard records sync through your own iCloud. Only you can see them — not us.

怎么备份学习记录？

「设置 → 数据 → 导出备份…」会生成一个 JSON 文件，可以存到「文件」App 或发给自己；需要时用「导入备份…」恢复。导入是合并，不会删掉本机已有的记录。

Settings → 数据 (Data) → 导出备份… (Export Backup) saves a JSON file you can keep in Files or send to yourself; 导入备份… (Import Backup) restores it. Importing merges and never deletes existing records.

朗读没有声音？

朗读走的是媒体音量，不受静音开关影响：先按音量键调大音量，再看看是不是连着蓝牙耳机或音箱。想要更自然的声音，可以在「系统设置 → 辅助功能 → 朗读内容 → 声音」里下载中文和英文的增强语音。

Read-aloud uses media volume (the silent switch doesn't affect it): turn the volume up and check for connected Bluetooth headphones or speakers. For more natural voices, download enhanced Chinese and English voices in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

购买和付款都由 Apple 处理，开发者看不到订单，也无法直接退款。请到 reportaproblem.apple.com 登录你的 Apple ID 申请。

Purchases are handled by Apple; we can't see orders or issue refunds. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

书里的内容能代替看医生吗？

不能。本书是科普读物，不构成医疗建议，也不能用于诊断或治疗。身体不适请就医；出现危险信号——比如突然口角歪斜、一侧手脚无力、说话不清、胸痛、呼吸困难——请立即拨打当地急救电话（中国大陆 120，美国 911）。

No. This is popular science, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you feel unwell, see a doctor; for warning signs such as sudden facial droop, one-sided weakness, slurred speech, chest pain or trouble breathing, call your local emergency number right away (120 in mainland China, 911 in the US).
