隐私政策

Privacy Policy · The Human Body: An Owner's Manual

生效日期 Effective date：2026-10-23

一句话：《人体使用手册》不收集任何个人数据。没有账号，没有服务器，没有广告，没有数据分析或追踪，也不含任何第三方 SDK。

In short: The Human Body: An Owner's Manual collects no personal data. No accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

保存在你设备上的数据 Data on your device

你的阅读进度、书签、高亮、笔记、自测成绩、记忆卡、实验记录和阅读设置，只保存在你自己的设备上。删除 App，或在「设置 → 数据 → 清空学习记录」里清空，这些数据就会被删除。

Your reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, quiz scores, flashcards, lab records and reading settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app, or using Settings → 数据 (Data) → 清空学习记录 (Clear Study Records), removes them.

iCloud 同步 iCloud sync

如果你的设备登录了 iCloud，App 会用 Apple 的 iCloud 键值存储，在你自己的 iPhone 和 iPad 之间同步上面这些学习记录。这些数据存放在你的 iCloud 账户里，受 Apple 隐私政策约束，开发者无法访问。不想同步，可以在系统设置里为本 App 关闭 iCloud。

If your device is signed in to iCloud, the app uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage to sync these study records between your own devices. The data lives in your iCloud account, is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, and cannot be accessed by us. You can turn off iCloud for this app in system Settings.

购买 Purchases

「完整版」通过 Apple App Store 购买，付款由 Apple 处理。我们收不到你的姓名、邮箱、Apple ID 或任何付款信息；App 只向 Apple 查询「是否已购买」，用来解锁内容。

The Full Book is purchased through the Apple App Store and payment is handled by Apple. We never receive your name, email, Apple ID or payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase exists in order to unlock content.

朗读、搜索与备份 Read-aloud, search and backups

  • 朗读使用设备自带的系统语音，在本机合成，文字不会发送给我们。
  • 全书搜索在本机进行；系统「聚焦搜索」里能搜到的章节、疾病和术语索引也只保存在你的设备上。
  • 只有你主动「导出备份」时才会生成备份文件，存到你自己选择的位置。
  • Read-aloud uses your device's built-in system voices; speech is synthesized on the device and no text is sent to us.
  • Search runs on the device; the Spotlight index of chapters, diseases and terms stays on your device.
  • Backup files are created only when you choose Export Backup, and saved where you choose.

网络 Network

全书内容都打包在 App 里，阅读不需要联网。只有 App Store 购买 / 恢复购买和 iCloud 同步会由系统联网完成。

All content is bundled in the app; reading works offline. Only App Store purchases/restores and iCloud sync use the network, through Apple's system services.

儿童 Children

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
